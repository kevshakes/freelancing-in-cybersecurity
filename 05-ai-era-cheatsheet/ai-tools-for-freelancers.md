# Using AI Tools to Freelance Faster

AI is both the threat you're selling protection against and a tool that can speed up your own freelance operations. Use it to move faster — never let it replace your judgment or go out under your name unreviewed.

## Prospecting

- Draft target-client lists by industry/size/region, then verify manually
- Summarize a prospective client's public footprint (website, LinkedIn, news) to personalize outreach
- Never scrape or use data in ways that violate a platform's terms of service or privacy law

## Proposal & content drafting

- Generate a first draft of a proposal, then rewrite the opening line yourself — this is the line clients notice most
- Use AI to tighten your bio/headline drafts, but keep facts and claims accurate — never let it invent a credential or number
- Draft social/LinkedIn post outlines, then add your own voice and real examples

## Report writing

- Turn raw scan output, notes, or findings into a first-draft client report structure
- Always personally verify every technical finding before it goes to a client — AI can misinterpret scan output
- Use AI to improve clarity for non-technical stakeholders, not to replace your analysis

## Guardrails — non-negotiable

- Never send an AI-drafted message to a client without reading and editing it yourself
- Never let AI tools touch client data without checking the tool's data handling policy first
- Disclose AI-assisted work where relevant, especially for reports or audits — trust is the product
- Don't rely on AI for security judgment calls (severity ratings, false positive/negative determination) — that's your expertise, and it's what you're being paid for
