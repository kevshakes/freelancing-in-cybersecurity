# ISACA Certifications Guide (incl. CCOA)

ISACA certifications carry strong weight with enterprise and compliance-focused clients — they signal you understand governance, risk, and audit, not just technical execution. This is especially valuable for freelancers positioning around compliance audits and GRC work.

## The core lineup

| Certification | Focus | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **CISA** — Certified Information Systems Auditor | IT audit, control, and assurance | Freelancers offering compliance/audit review services |
| **CISM** — Certified Information Security Manager | Security program management and governance | Freelancers moving toward security leadership/advisory roles |
| **CRISC** — Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control | IT risk identification and management | Risk assessment and GRC consulting services |
| **CGEIT** — Certified in the Governance of Enterprise IT | Enterprise IT governance | Advisory work with boards/senior leadership on IT governance |
| **CDPSE** — Certified Data Privacy Solutions Engineer | Privacy by design, data protection implementation | GDPR/data privacy readiness services — directly relevant to AI-era compliance work |

All five typically require a mix of exam plus verified work experience for full certification status — check current requirements on isaca.org before committing, as experience requirements and exam formats are updated periodically.

## CCOA — Certified Cybersecurity Operations Analyst (newer, highly relevant)

ISACA's CCOA is a newer, more hands-on credential worth watching closely if you're positioning around SOC-as-a-service, incident detection, or operational security work — it's a strong complement to the AI-era threat framing from tonight's session.

**Exam structure:** a 4-hour exam blending traditional multiple-choice questions with performance-based questions that require actually using open-source security tools — not just theory recall. This makes it a stronger practical signal than many traditional GRC-style certifications.

**Five domains covered:**
1. Technology Essentials — networking, operating systems, and infrastructure fundamentals
2. Cybersecurity Principles and Risk
3. Adversarial Tactics, Techniques and Procedures
4. Incident Detection and Response
5. Securing Assets

**Why it's worth watching:** it directly maps to the SOC-as-a-service and incident response skills covered tonight, and its hands-on performance-based format gives clients a stronger practical signal than a pure knowledge exam. Check isaca.org/credentialing/ccoa for the current exam window, cost, and prerequisites before enrolling, as these details are updated by ISACA periodically.

## How to position ISACA certs on LinkedIn/platforms

- Don't just list the acronym — add a one-line translation for non-technical clients (e.g. "CISA — independent audit and controls assurance")
- If in progress, say so: "Pursuing CCOA (ISACA) — hands-on SOC and incident response certification"
- Pair a GRC-focused ISACA cert with a technical one (e.g. CDPSE + AWS Security Specialty) if your services span both compliance and hands-on work

Sources: ISACA CCOA certification page (isaca.org/credentialing/ccoa) and NICCS training catalog — verify current details directly with ISACA before enrolling, as exam structure and pricing are subject to change.
