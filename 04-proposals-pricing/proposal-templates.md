# Proposal Templates

Four steps, every time: name their problem, show proof, propose a next step, price with confidence.

## Template 1 — Platform proposal (Upwork/Freelancer)

```
Hi [Client name],

[One sentence naming their exact problem, referencing their job post directly —
not a generic greeting.]

[1–2 sentences of proof: a relevant result, certification, or past project.
Link a writeup or portfolio item if you have one.]

For this project, I'd suggest [a clear, scoped next step — a short call,
a mini-audit, or a fixed-price starter package]. My rate for this scope
is [$X], and I can start [timeframe].

Happy to answer any questions before we begin.

[Your name]
```

## Template 2 — Fiverr-style fixed-scope offer

```
Service: [specific deliverable]
What's included: [bullet list — be exact, avoid "etc."]
Turnaround: [X days]
Price: [$X]
What I need from you to start: [access, target scope, documents]
```

## Template 3 — Short-form (for quick-turn platforms or DMs)

```
Saw you need [specific need]. I do exactly this — [1-line proof].
I can scope a [starter package] for [$X], delivered in [timeframe].
Want me to send a short outline?
```

## Proposal checklist before you hit send

- [ ] Does the opening line prove you read their post (not a template)?
- [ ] Is there one specific proof point, not a list of adjectives?
- [ ] Is the next step concrete (a call, a scoped deliverable — not "let's chat")?
- [ ] Is the price stated, not vague ("negotiable" reads as inexperienced)?
- [ ] Did you proofread for their company/project name (not a leftover placeholder)?
