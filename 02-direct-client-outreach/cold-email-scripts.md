# Cold Email Scripts

One problem, one proof point, one ask. Five sentences, not five paragraphs.

## Script 1 — General SMB outreach

```
Subject: Quick question about [Company]'s security setup

Hi [Name],

Noticed [specific observation — e.g. "you're growing your online store" /
"you recently posted about hiring"] — companies at that stage are a common
target for AI-generated phishing right now.

I help SMBs like yours run phishing simulations and close the gap before
it's exploited [1-line proof: cert, past result, or number of audits done].

Worth a 15-minute call to see if it's relevant for you?

[Your name]
[LinkedIn / portfolio link]
```

## Script 2 — Referencing a specific trigger event

```
Subject: Following up after [event/news — e.g. "the recent data breach news
in your industry"]

Hi [Name],

Saw the news about [specific trigger]. It's a good moment to double-check
[specific relevant risk area, e.g. "whether your team can spot an AI-generated
phishing attempt"].

I run [specific service] for companies your size — happy to send a short
outline of what a scoped review would look like, no obligation.

[Your name]
```

## Script 3 — Warm-ish (community / referral context)

```
Subject: [Mutual connection] suggested I reach out

Hi [Name],

[Mutual connection] mentioned you might be looking into [specific need].
I specialize in [service] — recently [proof point].

Would it help to see a quick outline of how I'd approach this for you?

[Your name]
```

## Rules for cold email

- Subject line: specific, never "Cybersecurity services" or "Quick question"
- One ask per email — don't offer three services in one message
- No attachments in the first message — link to a portfolio instead
- Follow up once, 4–5 days later, with new information (not just "just following up")
- Never claim urgency or use fear-based framing you can't back up — it reads as a scam, ironically
