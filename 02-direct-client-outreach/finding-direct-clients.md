# Finding Direct Clients (Without a Platform)

Platforms bring clients to you, at a cut. Direct clients pay you fully, but you have to find them. Here's where to actually look.

## 1. Your existing network (start here, always)

- ALX/AWS/ISACA community — alumni running businesses, hiring managers, fellow freelancers who overflow work to trusted contacts
- Past colleagues, classmates, lecturers — anyone who's seen you work
- Family/friends who run or work at small businesses — often the easiest first client, with realistic expectations

## 2. Local business communities

- Chamber of commerce and SME associations — often have digital/security concerns and no in-house expertise
- Co-working spaces and startup hubs — talk to founders directly, offer a free 15-minute security check-in as an icebreaker
- Local tech meetups and industry events — not just security ones; e-commerce, fintech, and SME-focused events often have your ideal clients in the room

## 3. Online, beyond platforms

- LinkedIn — see [`01-linkedin-glowup/content-posting-plan.md`](../01-linkedin-glowup/content-posting-plan.md) for how content attracts inbound interest
- Niche Slack/Discord/WhatsApp communities for founders, e-commerce operators, or specific industries
- Company "careers" or "about" pages — identify growing companies without a listed security hire, a strong sign they need freelance support

## 4. Referral systems (the highest-ROI channel over time)

- After every completed project, ask directly: "Do you know one other business that might need this kind of help?"
- Offer a small referral incentive (a discount on their next engagement, or a simple thank-you) — make it easy to say yes
- Stay visible to past clients even after the project ends — a quarterly check-in email keeps you top of mind

## Qualifying a lead before you pitch

Before reaching out, quickly check:
- Do they plausibly have the problem you solve (size, industry, public signals)?
- Do they plausibly have budget (funded startup, established SME, recent growth signal)?
- Is there a real, specific reason to reach out now (not just "they exist")?

Skipping this step is the biggest time-waster in direct outreach — a personalized message to the wrong lead still gets ignored.
