# LinkedIn Outreach Templates

Comment first, connect second, pitch third. Relationship before request.

## Step 1 — Comment (before you ever message)

Engage genuinely on 2–3 posts from your target client/company over a week or two. A thoughtful comment on their hiring post, product launch, or industry take builds recognition before you ever land in their inbox.

## Step 2 — Connection request note

```
Hi [Name] — enjoyed your post on [specific topic]. I work in cybersecurity,
focused on [your specialty], and would love to stay connected.
```

Keep it under 300 characters. No pitch at this stage.

## Step 3 — First DM (after they accept)

```
Thanks for connecting, [Name]! I noticed [specific company detail — recent
funding, hiring, product launch]. Curious how you're currently handling
[relevant security concern] — happy to share a quick perspective if useful,
no pitch intended.
```

## Step 4 — The pitch (only after some back-and-forth)

```
Since we've been chatting — I do [specific service] for companies like
[Company]. If it's ever useful, I'd be glad to send a short outline of
what a scoped [service] would look like for you.
```

## LinkedIn content that attracts inbound (do this in parallel)

- Post short breakdowns of publicly known incidents ("here's how an AI-generated deepfake scam works, and 3 signs to catch it")
- Share a sanitized before/after from a project (with client permission)
- Comment thoughtfully on posts from your target industry, not just other security people

## What not to do

- Don't pitch in the connection request — near-instant decline rate
- Don't mass-message identical scripts — personalize at least the first line
- Don't oversell certifications the client won't recognize — translate them into outcomes
