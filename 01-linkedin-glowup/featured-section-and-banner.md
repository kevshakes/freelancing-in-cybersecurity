# Featured Section & Banner Checklist

LinkedIn's Featured section is prime portfolio real estate — most freelancers leave it empty. Fix that before anything else.

## Banner image

- [ ] Custom banner (1584 x 396px), not the default LinkedIn gradient
- [ ] Include your specialty and a short value statement as text overlay (e.g. "Cybersecurity for the AI Era — Phishing Simulation · Compliance · SOC")
- [ ] Keep it on-brand with your platform bios and deck for consistency
- [ ] Avoid clutter — one clear message, readable at thumbnail size

## Featured section — what to pin (aim for 3-5 items)

1. **A write-up or case study** — link to your best GitHub write-up or a sanitized project summary
2. **A certification** — link to a verifiable credential page (not just an image)
3. **A LinkedIn post that performed well** — pin your best-performing content as social proof
4. **A service one-pager or portfolio PDF** — if you have one, even a simple one
5. **A short intro video** (optional but high-impact) — 60-90 seconds, tells clients what you do and how to start working with you

## Profile photo

- [ ] Real headshot, front-facing, plain or simple background
- [ ] Same photo used consistently across LinkedIn, GitHub, and platform profiles
- [ ] Professional but approachable — avoid overly formal studio shots if your niche is startups/SMBs

## Custom URL

- [ ] Update your LinkedIn URL to `linkedin.com/in/yourname` (not the default string of numbers) — cleaner to share in proposals and email signatures
