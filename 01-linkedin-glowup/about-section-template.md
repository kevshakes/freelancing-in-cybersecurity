# LinkedIn "About" Section Template

The About section is where clients decide whether to message you. Most people either leave it blank or paste their CV — both are missed opportunities.

## Structure (150-250 words)

```
[Hook — 1 sentence: a result, a moment, or a clear statement of who you help]

[Paragraph 1 — What you do: your specialty, who you serve, the problem you solve.
Be specific: "AI-driven phishing" not "cybersecurity threats in general".]

[Paragraph 2 — Proof: certifications, years of experience, number of
projects/audits/clients, any notable results. Use real numbers where you have them.]

[Paragraph 3 — How you work / what makes you different: your approach,
your values, or what clients can expect working with you.]

[Call to action — how to reach you, what to message you about.]
```

## Filled example

"Late one night, an AI-cloned voice nearly moved six figures out of a client's account. That's the world freelancers in this field now defend against.

I help small and mid-sized businesses close the gap between traditional security and AI-powered threats — phishing simulations, security awareness training, and compliance readiness reviews built for how attacks actually work today.

3 years in cybersecurity consulting. AWS Security Specialty certified. 12+ completed audits, zero missed findings on client re-tests. ALX Cybersecurity Cohort 1 alumni.

I work in plain language, deliver clear reports (not jargon), and always leave clients with a prioritized action list — not just a list of problems.

Open to project-based and retainer work. Message me or check the Featured section below for samples."

## Checklist

- [ ] Opens with a hook, not "I am a cybersecurity professional with..."
- [ ] Names the specific audience you serve
- [ ] Includes at least one real, verifiable number
- [ ] Ends with a clear call to action
- [ ] Free of unexplained jargon a non-technical client wouldn't understand
