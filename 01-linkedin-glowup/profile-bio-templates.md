# Profile Bio Templates

Fill in the brackets. Keep it under 150 words for platform profiles — clients skim.

## Template 1 — Service-first

I help [target client type, e.g. "SMEs and e-commerce brands"] [solve specific problem, e.g. "stop AI-driven phishing before it reaches an inbox"]. [X years / X completed projects] in [your specialty]. Certified in [top 1–2 certs]. [One proof point — a result, a number, a named skill]. Let's talk about your [specific need, e.g. "next security audit"].

**Example:**
"I help SMEs and e-commerce brands stop AI-driven phishing before it reaches an inbox. 3 years in security awareness and phishing simulation, AWS Security Specialty certified, 12 completed audits with zero missed findings on client re-tests. Let's talk about your next security awareness campaign."

## Template 2 — Story-first (for cold outreach / LinkedIn "About")

[One-line hook — a result or a moment]. That's the kind of problem I solve for [client type]. I specialize in [1–2 services], and I've [credibility marker — trained X people / certified in Y / built Z]. If you're dealing with [specific pain point], I'd like to help.

## Template 3 — Fiverr gig title + short bio

**Gig title:** "I will [specific outcome] using [method/tool]"
*(e.g. "I will run a phishing simulation campaign and train your team to spot AI-generated scams")*

**Short bio:** Certified [credential] with hands-on experience in [service]. Fast turnaround, clear reporting, no jargon — you'll know exactly what's at risk and what to fix.

## Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Name the specific problem you solve | Use "cybersecurity enthusiast" or "hard worker" |
| Include one measurable proof point | List every tool you've ever touched |
| Match your bio tone across platforms | Copy-paste a CV summary |
| End with a clear next step | End with no call to action |
