# LinkedIn Content & Engagement Plan (First 30 Days)

Visibility compounds. A glammed-up profile with zero activity still looks inactive to a client checking it today. This plan builds momentum without demanding hours a day.

## Weekly rhythm (aim for 2-3 posts/week + daily engagement)

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| Mon | Comment thoughtfully on 3-5 posts from target clients/industry accounts |
| Tue | Post: a breakdown of a public incident or concept (see prompts below) |
| Wed | Comment on 3-5 posts; engage with your own post's replies |
| Thu | Post: a quick tip, before/after, or lesson from a project/CTF |
| Fri | Comment on 3-5 posts; send 1-2 warm connection requests |
| Weekend | Optional: longer-form post (a mini case study or write-up teaser) |

## Post prompt ideas

- "Here's how an AI-generated deepfake scam works, and 3 signs to catch it"
- "3 things I check first in every phishing simulation I run"
- A sanitized before/after from a project (with client permission)
- A reaction to recent, verified security news — your take, not just a repost
- A short thread on a certification you're pursuing and what it's teaching you
- Behind-the-scenes: what a scoped security audit actually looks like, step by step

## Rules for content that builds trust (not noise)

- Always cite real, current sources for any statistic — don't repeat unverified numbers
- Keep language plain — you're often writing for non-technical decision-makers, not just security peers
- Show your work: process and reasoning build more trust than polished conclusions alone
- Engage back — reply to every comment on your posts within 24 hours

## Tracking (simple)

Keep a running note of: post date, topic, engagement (likes/comments/shares), and any DMs or leads it generated. Review monthly — double down on what resonates.
