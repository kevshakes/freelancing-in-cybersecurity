# Glossary

Plain-language definitions for acronyms and terms used across this repo. Written for a mixed-experience audience — if you already know these, skip this file.

**AI red-teaming** — Deliberately testing an AI system or chatbot to see if it can be manipulated into unsafe, biased, or harmful behavior.

**CCOA (Certified Cybersecurity Operations Analyst)** — An ISACA certification focused on hands-on SOC and incident response skills, using real open-source tools in the exam. See [07-certifications](./07-certifications).

**CDPSE (Certified Data Privacy Solutions Engineer)** — An ISACA certification focused on building data privacy into systems and processes.

**CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker)** — An EC-Council certification focused on offensive security techniques and penetration testing concepts.

**CGEIT (Certified in the Governance of Enterprise IT)** — An ISACA certification focused on aligning IT strategy with business governance.

**CISA (Certified Information Systems Auditor)** — An ISACA certification focused on auditing, control, and assurance of information systems.

**CISM (Certified Information Security Manager)** — An ISACA certification focused on security program management and governance.

**CISSP (Certified Information Systems Security Professional)** — An ISC2 certification, broad and senior-level, covering security architecture, operations, and management.

**Compliance audit** — A review that checks whether an organization meets a specific standard or regulation (for example, data privacy law or an information security standard).

**CRISC (Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control)** — An ISACA certification focused on identifying and managing IT risk.

**CTF (Capture The Flag)** — A practice competition where participants solve security challenges to find hidden "flags," used to demonstrate hands-on skill.

**Deepfake** — AI-generated audio, video, or images designed to convincingly impersonate a real person.

**GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance)** — The combined discipline of setting policy (governance), managing risk, and meeting regulatory requirements.

**Incident response** — The process of detecting, containing, and recovering from a security incident after it has occurred.

**OSCP (Offensive Security Certified Professional)** — A hands-on penetration testing certification from OffSec, respected for its practical exam format.

**Pentest (Penetration test)** — An authorized, simulated attack on a system to find exploitable weaknesses before a real attacker does.

**Phishing simulation** — A controlled, authorized test that sends realistic fake phishing messages to staff to measure and improve their ability to spot real ones.

**SOC (Security Operations Center)** — A team or service that monitors an organization's systems for security threats and responds to alerts, often on an ongoing basis.

**SOC-as-a-service** — SOC monitoring delivered as an outsourced, often remote service, common for organizations too small for their own in-house SOC.
